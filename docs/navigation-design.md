# Customer navigation · 2026-09-09

The information hierarchy follows the visitor's task rather than the list of demo features.

| Location | Primary purpose | Secondary content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Find an apartment or enter resident services | Three example homes, optional guided matching, area introduction |
| Homes | Filter, shortlist and compare | Apartment details and application tracking through the footer |
| Apartment | Inspect images, plan, 3D or tour; start application | House facts, systems, parking, room schedule, finishes and neighbourhood in labelled disclosures |
| Resident services | Choose a topic | Home and maintenance; payments and bookings; moving and documents |
| My home | Personal account overview | Existing payments, bookings, documents and energy views |
| Footer | Stable secondary navigation | Concept presentation, staff demo and reference apartments |

Header: three navigation destinations (Homes, Areas, For residents), with language, favourites and My home as utilities. The apartment page has one highlighted application action. Save/share/compare are grouped under More actions. Cost calculation remains within building information. The assistant appears only in resident workflows and does not overlap property imagery or search results.

No service routes or property information were removed. Repeated promotional sections were removed from the homepage and repeated apartment cards from the detail page. Plans, models and tours remain unfurnished on first opening. Finnish, English and Swedish use the same hierarchy.

Validation: successful production build; browser checks cover the first screen, navigation, resident grouping, apartment disclosures and responsive layout. These checks do not constitute a full accessibility audit or WebGL rendering review.
